# Sweep 1: 22 titles' first boot, headless (Qualcomm's driver)

The first run of `tools/native/sweep.sh` (TESTING-GAMES.md, "A sweep"):
the images on the Odin2's SD card that hadn't been run in HakuXP, each
booted once on an empty disk, to group them by how they end and pick what
to fix. It was stopped after 22 of 39 images (09:31-10:07) when it turned
out it ran on the wrong GPU driver.

**Device:** AYN Odin2 (QCS8550, Adreno 740), Android 13
(Odin2_V1.0.0.380_20250815_111346_user).
**GPU driver:** Qualcomm's system driver, `vk: driver "Qualcomm
Technologies Inc. Adreno Vulkan Driver" (id 8) Driver Build: 1a285a84ae`,
**not** Turnip: the runs passed `vkdriver=<path to vulkan.purple.so>`,
which `xboxrun` ignored without a word (fixed afterwards, see below). The
app uses Turnip by default on this device.
**Runner:** `xboxrun` from the native build of 2026-11-15 20:12, stripped,
MD5 `9dcfd1840d425c97d0284b13f80a7144`. No APK involved.
**Each run:** 90 s (`NC_TIMEOUT`), `hang=30`, `capture=300,900,1800`, an
empty disk deleted afterwards, Start and A pressed alternately every 5 s
from 15 s (`15000:start:150,20000:a:150,...,85000:start:150`).
**XBE MD5s:** read from each image on the device
(`tools/native/xbehash.py --adb`). Images not hashed whole.
**Evidence:** `build/logs/xboxrun/2026-11-16-sweep1/` (`N.out`,
`N/frames/`, `results.tsv`); `python tools/native/sweep.py <that folder>`.

## Groups

- **Ran to the limit (16):** 1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 8, 9, 13-21. Nothing in the
  core stopped them; what they drew is another matter (below).
- **Relaunched into an XBE (4):** Cel Damage, Counter-Strike and NASCAR
  Heat 2002 into `default.xbe` again, Forza into `forza.xbe` (its
  `default.xbe` is a launcher, `584C8014` `XL3278870W`). The app follows a
  relaunch (a new process with `relaunch`); `xboxrun` didn't. Now it does
  with `NC_FOLLOW=N` (below).
- **Hang (1):** DroneZ, after its nickname screen: a thread polling with
  `NtYieldExecution` starved a lower-priority one. Fixed:
  [the DroneZ record](2026-11-16-dronez-yield.md).
- **Guest stopped (1):** Fallout 2 CE (homebrew) at RDMSR. Fixed:
  [the Fallout 2 CE record](2026-11-16-fallout2ce-rdmsr.md).
- **Unimplemented imports:** 15 of 18 titles that logged them import
  XAPI's I/O driver-model functions (`IoStartPacket`, `IofCompleteRequest`,
  `IoMarkIrpMustComplete`, `IoStartNextPacket`, `IoInvalidDeviceRequest`;
  6 also `IofCallDriver`, `IoBuildSynchronousFsdRequest`,
  `IoSynchronousFsdRequest`), 2 the I/O completion ports and
  `NtWriteFileGather`. No run stopped on one.

## The pictures

Most captures are black or one plain colour, even where the title drew
thousands of draws a second (Crash Bandicoot, Fuzion Frenzy, MX vs. ATV),
while others show menus and video correctly (Battlefield 2, Burnout 3,
Hello Kitty, Halo 2). The gamma pass wasn't involved (the ramps were
identity, except Battlefield 2's and Burnout 3's, whose frames are right).
RalliSport Challenge 2 showed a black picture on this same system driver
in the app on 2026-11-15, and the 2026-11-15 headless Halo frames
("very dark") were on it too. So these pictures say little about HakuXP
on Turnip; sweep 2 reruns the set on Turnip.

## Fixes that came out of it

| Problem | Fix | Checked |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `vkdriver=<library path>` ignored by `xboxrun`, silently | `xboxrun` makes it `DIR\|LIB\|HOOKS` (hooks beside the runner); the loader logs a spec it can't use | Turnip loads (`vk: driver "PurpleVK public driver" (id 18)`, kgsl priority 7) with both forms |
| A relaunch ends a headless run | `NC_FOLLOW=N`: `xboxrun` starts itself again with `relaunch` and `ec=`, the time left, the frames so far in `frames.<k>/` | Cel Damage: relaunched, ran on 63 s; Counter-Strike's new process died of a pending SIGPROF (exit 155), fixed (`signal(SIGPROF, SIG_IGN)` before `execv`); rerun (runner MD5 `3d532fff...`, Turnip, `NC_FOLLOW=2`, 120 s): Counter-Strike's menu, Training, the relaunch, then the training match's tips and pause menu at 30 fps; Forza's launcher into `forza.xbe` (`4D53006E` `MS11070W`), its profile keyboard and starting grid; NASCAR Heat 2002's menus; Cel Damage into a race. All four to the limit (exit 5) |
| DroneZ hang | `NtYieldExecution` yields to lower priorities | see its record |
| Fallout 2 CE at RDMSR | RDMSR/WRMSR emulated | see its record |
