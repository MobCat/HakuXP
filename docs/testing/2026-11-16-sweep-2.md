# Sweep 2: 39 titles' first boot, headless, on Turnip

Sweep 1 again with what it led to: Turnip actually loaded, relaunches
followed, and the DroneZ, Fallout 2 CE and crash fixes in the runner. All
39 images on the Odin2's SD card that hadn't been run in HakuXP, in two
parts: 1-19 at 10:31-10:58, then the app was opened on the device and the
sweep stopped itself; 20-39 at 11:02-11:32 (`SWEEP_FROM=20`, same folder).

**Device:** AYN Odin2 (QCS8550, Adreno 740), Android 13.
**GPU driver:** PurpleVK 26.3.0-devel (git-62ac221a33), Turnip T30, the
app's custom package with its hook libraries and `meta.json` beside the
runner (`vk: driver "PurpleVK public driver" (id 18)` in every run, kgsl
priority 7 as in the app).
**Runner:** `xboxrun` built 2026-11-16 10:08 (stripped MD5
`3d532fff3054e4ba613003ced683d916`). No APK involved.
**Each run:** as sweep 1 (90 s, `hang=30`, `capture=300,900,1800`, empty
disk, Start/A every 5 s from 15 s), plus `NC_FOLLOW=2`: a relaunch's new
run shares the 90 s, its frames count from 0 again and the earlier ones
are in `frames.1/`.
**XBE MD5s:** read from each image on the device (`xbehash.py --adb`):
the image's `default.xbe` (for Forza and TransWorld Snowboarding, the one
before the relaunch; the IDs shown are the XBE relaunched into).
**Evidence:** `build/logs/xboxrun/2026-11-16-sweep2/`.

## Leads

- **Xbox Music Mixer (USA), `4D53005A` `MS09004A`:** halts 0.3 s in
  (`stop: guest executed HLT`, after `FEX: Invalid or Unknown instruction:
  0x0`), no frame. Before it: it probes the disc through `\??\CDROM0:`
  (`default.xbe`, `video_ts\video_ts.ifo`, `track01.cda`, ...), each an
  "unknown device"; it changes the protection of page 0x31A000 twice; it
  reads 0xFFFE001A-0xFFFE001F (the flash) as zeros. The unknown device was
  a HakuXP bug: `canon` (native/kernel/io.c) expanded only drive letters,
  so the kernel's own `\??\CdRom0:` link never worked. Fixed after this
  sweep; rerun 11:40 (runner MD5 `725d9024...`, Turnip,
  `build/logs/xboxrun/2026-11-16-fixes/musicmixer-*.out`): `\??\CDROM0:`
  now resolves, it finds `default.xbe` and stops probing, then
  `XcVerifyPKCS1Signature` passes. It still halts the same way: code at
  0x2419A0 (`.text`) makes the first page of `.rdata` (0x31A000, where the
  kernel thunks usually are) writable and executable and back, twice, then
  something reads the flash at 0xFFFE001F, 0xFFFE001C and 0xFFFE001A (all
  zero here), and the next guest code is invalid. Next: disassemble around
  0x2419A0 and the flash reads (the XBE is in the scratch folder only; its
  `SANDRD`/`SANDRW` sections look like a protection layer); what the Xbox's
  flash holds there.
- **Re-Volt (XL00100W.iso), `584C0001` `XL00100`:** its title screen at
  all three captures, 400-1200 fps (about 100,000 draws a second): its
  swaps don't wait for a vblank, and it doesn't leave the title on the
  scripted Start presses, which reach it (`gamepad 0: buttons 0000 ->
  0010`).
- **Halo (Japan), `4D530004` `MS00406J`:** black at all three captures at
  59.8 fps (sweep 1 on Qualcomm's driver: the same). Halo (USA) showed its
  intro video in the app 6 s after starting (10:26, its map cache already
  on the app's disk). A first boot on an empty disk may be building the
  map cache behind a black screen; to look at in the app.
- **Fallout 2 CE:** 8.4 fps (8.6 in its own fix run): its waits.
- **Slow loads:** Forza (2.7 fps at worst), WarPath (0.7), TransWorld
  Snowboarding (6.9), Blazing Angels (0.4): not looked at.
